Your Privacy Matters to Us

We are straightforward about what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. No legal jargon — just plain English.

Golden Oak Painting  ·  Bothell, WA  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Golden Oak Painting ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website at www.goldenoakpainting.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site or contact us, how we use that information, and your rights regarding your data.

We are a small, locally owned painting contractor based in Snohomish County, Washington. We do not sell your information. We do not share it with data brokers. We collect only what we need to respond to your inquiries and provide our services.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you fill out our contact or estimate request form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address
  • Service type requested
  • Project description and any details you choose to share

This information is submitted through our contact form and is used solely to respond to your request and schedule your free estimate. We do not add you to marketing lists without your explicit consent.

Website Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to collect anonymous data about how visitors use our site. This includes:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • How you arrived at our site (search engine, direct, referral)
  • General geographic location (city or region level — not your specific address)
  • Device type, browser, and screen size

This data is aggregated and anonymous. It helps us understand which pages are most useful and improve our website. Google Analytics data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored in your browser — for the following purposes:

  • Analytics cookies: Set by Google Analytics to track site usage (as described above).
  • Functionality cookies: Used to remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your ability to use our website or submit an estimate request.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your estimate request or inquiry
  • Schedule and confirm on-site estimate appointments
  • Communicate about your painting project
  • Provide quotes, proposals, and project updates
  • Follow up after your project is complete

We use analytics data to understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. We do not use any collected data for automated decision-making or profiling.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited data with trusted third-party services that help us operate our website and business:

  • Web3Forms — processes our contact form submissions. Your form data is transmitted to us via their API. View their Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — analyzes anonymous website traffic as described above.
  • Google Tag Manager — manages analytics scripts on our site.
  • Cloudflare — provides website performance and security services. View their Privacy Policy.

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and may only use it for the specific services they provide to us. We do not share your information with any advertising networks or data brokers.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. Our website is served over HTTPS, which encrypts data transmitted between your browser and our server. Contact form submissions are transmitted securely via Web3Forms.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we work hard to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and related communications for as long as needed to complete your project and fulfill our warranty obligations (up to 3 years after project completion). Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics' default retention settings. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

As a resident of Washington State, you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request that inaccurate information be corrected
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal information
  • Opt out — opt out of any marketing communications from us

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites such as Google Reviews, Facebook, and Instagram. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before submitting any personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
